Privacy-first bulk sender

Send personalized emails, at scale

One CSV. One template. Every recipient gets their own version — with their own files — and everything disappears when done.

What you'll need

📄

A CSV file

Exported from Excel or Google Sheets. Just emails, or emails + names, companies, files.

Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo — any SMTP. You'll need an App Password, not your normal password.

📎

Files (optional)

One per recipient, if you need. Different file for each person is supported.

Get your App Password

Your normal email password won't work here. Google (and most providers) require a separate App Password for third-party apps. Here's how to get one:

1

Open your Google Account

Go to myaccount.google.com and sign in with your Gmail account.

2

Open the Security tab

On the left sidebar, click Security.

3

Turn on 2-Step Verification

If it's not already on, enable it. App Passwords only work with 2-Step Verification active.

4

Find "App Passwords"

Search for App Passwords in the Security page's search bar, or go directly to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords.

5

Create a new App Password

Type a name like "Bulk Email Sender" and click Create.

6

Copy the 16-character password

Google shows a password like abcd efgh ijkl mnop. Copy it. You won't see it again.

1

2

3

Create a new app password

Under "App passwords" → "Create a new app password".

4

Copy the password

You won't see it again.

2

Copy the password
